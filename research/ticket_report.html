Per-task search on LIBERO-Object: FiLM-z vs Golden-Ticket noise (SmolVLA)

Baseline round after steering round 2 and the ICL optimization. Golden Ticket (GT) improves the same checkpoint by replacing the random initial flow noise with one fixed, searched noise tensor. Here the same CEM, budget and inits search three spaces: our FiLM z (d = 8), an 8-dim subspace of the noise, and the full 1600-dim noise (GT). Question: at a small, equal budget, which space gives better held-out success?

Setup

  • Policy HuggingFaceVLA/smolvla_libero (the GT checkpoint), suite libero_object, tasks t0, t7, t9, t4, t2 (as steer2), replanning every step (n_action_steps = 1, also GT's setting).
  • Protocol (per task, as steer2): each candidate runs once on each search init 0–7 (rate = successes / 8; ties by mean steps of the successes). The best candidate of a chain is re-run on held-out inits 10–39 × 2 reps (n = 60), paired with z = 0 on the same (init, rep). Held-out flow-noise seeds are steer2's (seed + init + 1000·rep).
  • FiLM-z8: as steer2 / ICL. FiLM on the action-expert MLP outputs of layers 7, 15, 23, 31, [γ;β] = α·W·z, random W (seed 0), α = 0.3, z ∈ [−1.5, 1.5]⁸. Flow noise is random but paired per (init, chain).
  • noise-z8: fixed initial noise x₀ = U·z at every denoising call, U = random orthonormal 1600×8 columns × √200 (so z ~ N(0, I) gives x₀ entries ~ N(0, 1)), z ∈ [−3, 3]⁸. noise-1600: U = I, i.e. GT's ticket. With a fixed x₀ the policy is deterministic; z = 0 is the zero-noise policy, not the base policy.
  • CEM (all arms): population 6, 2 elites, mean/σ update σ ← max(½σ + ½·std(elites), 0.1), start N(0, σ₀²) with σ₀ = 0.5 (FiLM) or 1 (noise, the prior), 0.1 grid. Candidate 0 = z = 0. Budget 48 candidates (8 generations) = 384 search episodes, as the steer2 grid (48 z × 8 inits). A chain stops early when a candidate reaches 8/8.
  • Machine: local RTX 4090 laptop (Sol was in maintenance), two CEM chains (repeats) per (task, arm) for FiLM-z8 and noise-z8, one for noise-1600 (t0, t7 only). steer2 numbers are from Sol A100s; rollouts are not bit-reproducible across machines, so steer2 values are a reference, not a pair.
  • Code: research/ticket.py, research/inject/noise.py, queue experiments/libero/ticket/local.sh, figures research/plot_ticket.py.

Results

Fixed noise can freeze the policy (t0)

With replanning every step, a fixed x₀ makes the policy a deterministic map from observation to action. On t0 most fixed tickets stall: the arm reaches the scene, then barely moves until the time limit. Check on t0 inits 0–3 (scratch script, 1600-dim tickets, end-effector motion averaged over the last 50 steps):

Noise injection itself was checked (per-sample, deterministic, changes the actions), so this is a property of the fixed-noise setting, not a bug. It matches the search logs: zero noise scores 0/8 on t0 and t4, and most generation-0 noise draws score 0/8, while on t7 and t9 fixed noise works about as well as random noise. FiLM z keeps the random flow noise and does not have this failure mode.

Takeaways

FiLM-z8 is the more reliable space, not uniformly the better one. Mean held-out over the 10 chains (5 tasks × 2): FiLM-z8 0.89, noise-z8 0.79, z = 0 0.71. FiLM improves on z = 0 in 6/10 chains (sign test p < 0.05) and never gets worse (worst chain 0.67). noise-z8 improves in 5/10 chains but is significantly worse than z = 0 in 2 (t0 r1 0.10, t4 r1 0.20).

  • By task. t0: FiLM 0.95 / 0.85 vs noise 0.73 / 0.10. t7: noise wins clearly (1.00 / 0.97 vs FiLM 0.67 / 0.70). t9: both near the ceiling (z = 0 0.88). t4 and t2 are saturated, except the failed noise chain on t4.
  • Noise failure mode. On t0 and t4, zero noise and most prior samples score 0/8 (frozen policy, see above). CEM then has no gradient: t0 noise-z8 r1 and t0 noise-1600 used all 48 candidates at 0/8, and the pick falls back to candidate 0 (zero noise, held-out 0.10). Full 1600-dim noise is no better than the 8-dim subspace at this budget (t0 0.10, t7 0.97).
  • FiLM failure mode. t7 FiLM chains reach 8/8 on the search inits but only 0.67–0.70 held-out: the pick overfits 8 noisy episodes. The steer2 grid pick for the same W reached 0.90 on Sol, so better z exist in the space; re-evaluating the top-k candidates before picking is the obvious fix.
  • Budget. 48 candidates × 8 episodes, far below GT's ~1000 tickets × several envs. The claim supported here is limited to small budgets.
  • Caveats. Two chains per (task, arm); local RTX 4090, not paired with the Sol steer2 runs. Noise arms are deterministic, so their two held-out reps per init differ only by env randomness (≈ 30 effective inits), and their sign tests overstate significance. CEM draws use the same RNG stream across arms.
