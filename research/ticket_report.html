Per-task search on LIBERO-Object: FiLM-z vs Golden-Ticket noise (SmolVLA)

Baseline round after steering round 2 and the ICL optimization. Golden Ticket (GT) improves the same checkpoint by replacing the random initial flow noise with one fixed, searched noise tensor. Here the same CEM, budget and inits search three spaces: our FiLM z (d = 8), an 8-dim subspace of the noise, and the full 1600-dim noise (GT). Question: at a small, equal budget, which space gives better held-out success?

Setup

  • Policy HuggingFaceVLA/smolvla_libero (the GT checkpoint), suite libero_object, tasks t0, t7, t9, t4, t2 (as steer2), replanning every step (n_action_steps = 1, also GT's setting).
  • Protocol (per task, as steer2): each candidate runs once on each search init 0–7 (rate = successes / 8; ties by mean steps of the successes). The best candidate of a chain is re-run on held-out inits 10–39 × 2 reps (n = 60), paired with z = 0 on the same (init, rep). Held-out flow-noise seeds are steer2's (seed + init + 1000·rep).
  • FiLM-z8: as steer2 / ICL. FiLM on the action-expert MLP outputs of layers 7, 15, 23, 31, [γ;β] = α·W·z, random W (seed 0), α = 0.3, z ∈ [−1.5, 1.5]⁸. Flow noise is random but paired per (init, chain).
  • noise-z8: fixed initial noise x₀ = U·z at every denoising call, U = random orthonormal 1600×8 columns × √200 (so z ~ N(0, I) gives x₀ entries ~ N(0, 1)), z ∈ [−3, 3]⁸. noise-1600: U = I, i.e. GT's ticket. With a fixed x₀ the policy is deterministic; z = 0 is the zero-noise policy, not the base policy.
  • CEM (all arms): population 6, 2 elites, mean/σ update σ ← max(½σ + ½·std(elites), 0.1), start N(0, σ₀²) with σ₀ = 0.5 (FiLM) or 1 (noise, the prior), 0.1 grid. Candidate 0 = z = 0. Budget 48 candidates (8 generations) = 384 search episodes, as the steer2 grid (48 z × 8 inits). A chain stops early when a candidate reaches 8/8.
  • Machine: local RTX 4090 laptop (Sol was in maintenance), one chain per (task, arm). steer2 numbers are from Sol A100s; rollouts are not bit-reproducible across machines, so steer2 values are a reference, not a pair.
  • Code: research/ticket.py, research/inject/noise.py, queue experiments/libero/ticket/local.sh, figures research/plot_ticket.py.
