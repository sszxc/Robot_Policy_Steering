FiLM-z8 on SmolVLA: coupled vs split γ/β projection (LIBERO-Object)

Follow-up to the per-task search (ticket) and steering round 2. The FiLM projection used so far maps every z coordinate to both the scale γ and the shift β. Here a second projection keeps them apart, and the two are compared side by side at equal parameter-space energy. Both projections are kept; neither is the default.

Setup

  • FiLM on the action-expert MLP outputs x of layers 7, 15, 23, 31: x' = (1 + γ) ⊙ x + β, same γ, β for all action tokens and denoising steps. α = 0.3, d = 8, W seed 0 for both projections.
  • coupled (--w random, as in all earlier rounds): [γ; β] = α·W·z, W ∈ ℝ^{2H×8} Gaussian / √8, so each z_i moves both γ and β.
  • split (--w split): γ = α·Wγ·z[0:4], β = α·Wβ·z[4:8], each block Gaussian / √4. For the same per-coordinate z distribution, every γ and β entry has the same variance as under coupled, so ‖γ‖² + ‖β‖² matches (parameter-space parity). Along an axis, split puts all of that energy into γ or into β; coupled spreads it half and half.
  • Why γ may be weak at this parity: the MLP outputs are small (mean x² ≈ 0.012 at layers 7–23, ≈ 0.035 at layer 31, from the steer PCA fits), so γ ⊙ x is ~1/9 of β in amplitude for the same parameter size. γ is not centred (γ ⊙ mean(x) also shifts the output); that is left for later.
  • Open loop (research/openloop.py --axes): 96 saved policy inputs per task (t0, t7), fixed flow noise, z = c·√8·e_k for every axis k.
  • Closed-loop grid (research/run.py): t0 and t7, z = c·√8·e_k for all 8 axes, c ∈ {±0.5, ±1, ±2}, inits 0–7, one episode each, flow noise paired by init; both projections on the same z points. The best grid z per projection (split: best γ axis and best β axis separately) is re-run on held-out inits 10–39 × 2 reps, paired with the ticket round's z = 0.
  • CEM (research/ticket.py): the ticket FiLM arm repeated with --w split, all else equal (5 tasks, 2 chains, pop 6, 2 elites, σ₀ 0.5, box ±1.5, budget 48, held-out inits 10–39 × 2). Coupled FiLM-z8, noise-z8 and z = 0 are the ticket round's runs on the same machine.
  • Machine: local RTX 4090 laptop, B = 12 envs, as the ticket round. Queue experiments/libero/film_split/local.sh, figures research/plot_film_split.py.
