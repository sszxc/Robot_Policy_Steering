FiLM-z8 on SmolVLA: coupled vs split γ/β projection (LIBERO-Object)

Follow-up to the per-task search (ticket) and steering round 2. The FiLM projection used so far maps every z coordinate to both the scale γ and the shift β. Here a second projection keeps them apart, and the two are compared side by side at equal parameter-space energy. Both projections are kept; neither is the default.

Setup

  • FiLM on the action-expert MLP outputs x of layers 7, 15, 23, 31: x' = (1 + γ) ⊙ x + β, same γ, β for all action tokens and denoising steps. α = 0.3, d = 8, W seed 0 for both projections.
  • coupled (--w random, as in all earlier rounds): [γ; β] = α·W·z, W ∈ ℝ^{2H×8} Gaussian / √8, so each z_i moves both γ and β.
  • split (--w split): γ = α·Wγ·z[0:4], β = α·Wβ·z[4:8], each block Gaussian / √4. For the same per-coordinate z distribution, every γ and β entry has the same variance as under coupled, so ‖γ‖² + ‖β‖² matches (parameter-space parity). Along an axis, split puts all of that energy into γ or into β; coupled spreads it half and half.
  • Why γ may be weak at this parity: the MLP outputs are small (mean x² ≈ 0.012 at layers 7–23, ≈ 0.035 at layer 31, from the steer PCA fits), so γ ⊙ x is ~1/9 of β in amplitude for the same parameter size. γ is not centred (γ ⊙ mean(x) also shifts the output); that is left for later.
  • Open loop (research/openloop.py --axes): 96 saved policy inputs per task (t0, t7), fixed flow noise, z = c·√8·e_k for every axis k.
  • Closed-loop grid (research/run.py): t0 and t7, z = c·√8·e_k for all 8 axes, c ∈ {±0.5, ±1, ±2}, inits 0–7, one episode each, flow noise paired by init; both projections on the same z points. The best grid z per projection (split: best γ axis and best β axis separately) is re-run on held-out inits 10–39 × 2 reps, paired with the ticket round's z = 0.
  • CEM (research/ticket.py): the ticket FiLM arm repeated with --w split, all else equal (5 tasks, 2 chains, pop 6, 2 elites, σ₀ 0.5, box ±1.5, budget 48, held-out inits 10–39 × 2). Coupled FiLM-z8, noise-z8 and z = 0 are the ticket round's runs on the same machine.
  • Machine: local RTX 4090 laptop, B = 12 envs, as the ticket round. Queue experiments/libero/film_split/local.sh, figures research/plot_film_split.py.

Takeaways

At parameter-space parity, the γ half of split is inert, and split's β half does what coupled does. Here FiLM-z8 acts as a shift of the residual stream. Whether γ helps with an energy budget it can actually use is still open: this parity never gave it one.

  • Open loop. At c = 1 a γ axis moves the first action by 0.021 / 0.026 (t0 / t7) and a β axis by 0.19 / 0.24, a ratio of ≈ 1/9, which matches RMS(x). Even at c = 4, γ (0.08–0.10) stays far below the flow-noise spread (0.35–0.42). Coupled sits at ≈ β/√2 (0.14–0.15), as expected if it is ~99% β in residual energy.
  • Grid. γ axes are flat over every scale: t0 0.44–0.56 and t7 0.44–0.69, against z = 0 at 0.50 / 0.38 (8 episodes per point). Their paired eef-path change is at or below the noise reference (t0 20.6–24.3 cm vs 24.4; t7 13.7–19.6 vs 21.2). β axes are steep: only c = ±0.5 helps, |c| = 1 already hurts (0.25), and |c| = 2 gives 0. Coupled falls between the two (|c| = 2: 0.02–0.19).
  • Held-out of the grid picks. Coupled and β picks transfer: t0 0.92 and 0.80, t7 0.78 and 0.90, against z = 0 at 0.42 / 0.43 (all p < 1e-4). γ picks do not: t0 0.35 (p = 0.34) and t7 0.55 (p = 0.12). Their grid scores (0.75, 0.88) came from picking the best of 8-episode cells.
  • CEM. Split and coupled tie. Mean held-out over 5 tasks × 2 chains is 0.90 for split and 0.89 for coupled. Paired per episode (same init, noise and chain index), split wins 42 and loses 36 (p = 0.57). By task, split / coupled: t0 0.87 / 0.90, t7 0.84 / 0.68, t9 0.93 / 0.91, t4 0.90 / 0.98, t2 0.98 / 1.00. Two chains of the same arm differ about as much as the two arms do (t7 split 0.92 vs 0.77, t4 split 0.98 vs 0.82).
  • Where CEM puts z. In split's best z, ‖z_γ‖ (0.37–1.63) is about the same size as ‖z_β‖. CEM never pulls γ toward 0, because γ has no effect on success, so split CEM is in effect a 4-d β search with √2 larger β per coordinate.
  • Pairing holds. The z = 0 grid episodes are bitwise identical between the two projections, and in every chain split candidate 0 matches coupled.
  • Not run yet. Residual-space parity (γ scaled by 1/RMS(x) per layer, so a γ axis moves actions as much as a β axis) and centred γ (γ ⊙ (x − mean x), which drops the hidden shift γ ⊙ mean x).
